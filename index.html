Why I'm doing this

Because a good start to the school year shouldn't depend on what a family can afford.

My family has spent generations working in education right here in the Quad Cities, and I've seen firsthand how strong our local schools are. Students across our community have real opportunities in front of them, but that only holds true when they walk in the door with what they need on day one.

This drive is my way of making sure that's true for as many kids as possible this year, and I can't do it alone. I'm asking this community to collaborate with me the same way it always has: by showing up for its kids.

— PT Boeye
